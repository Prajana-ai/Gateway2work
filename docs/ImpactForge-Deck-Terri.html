The Challenge

The Post-Treatment Cliff

Individuals exiting substance use treatment or incarceration face a critical gap between recovery and sustainable employment. Current tools aren't built for this.

📄

Fragmented Systems

Spreadsheets, legacy tools like Apricot, and disconnected EHRs make coordinated case management nearly impossible.

⚠

Compliance Risk

HIPAA and 42 CFR Part 2 substance use data requires strict protections that general tools don't provide.

📊

No Outcome Visibility

Funders and programs can't measure placement rates, IPS fidelity, or Medicaid encounter billing without manual work.

2027 Medicaid Work Requirements — Clients must log 80 hours/month of employment activity. Programs need tools to track and report compliance now.

The Solution

Gateway2Work unifies every step of the workforce development journey into a single, compliant platform.

Trust & Compliance

Built for HIPAA & 42 CFR Part 2

Compliance isn't an add-on — it's the architecture. Two-zone data separation protects your clients and your organization.

🔒 Protected Data Zone

PII, PHI, and 42 CFR Part 2 substance use data. Encrypted at rest via pgcrypto. Audit triggers on every access. Consent-gated endpoints.

Client Demographics Referral Details Raw Assessments Encounter Notes Audit Logs

📊 Application Data Zone

De-identified operational data safe for dashboards, reports, and analytics. No FK constraints to protected zone — linked by surrogate UUIDs only.

Workforce Plans Employer Directory Assessment Summaries Workflows Form Templates

Security Posture
