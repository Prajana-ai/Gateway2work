Step 1

Referral & Intake

Participants enter via secure EHR feeds or direct partner referrals.

  • Secure Ingestion: CSV/API feeds from BHA clinics, jails, or courts.
  • Duplicate Detection: Fuzzy matching on Name and DOB.
  • Status Tracking: Transitions from Pending to Active upon intake completion.
  • Consent: 42 CFR Part 2 and HIPAA consent captured at start.

Step 2

Comprehensive Assessment

The "TSRG Three" assessments build a multi-dimensional profile of the participant.

🛠️

Skills Assessment

Baseline for digital literacy, soft skills, and vocational experience.

⭐

Interests Assessment

Identifies preferred sectors (Healthcare, Skilled Trades, etc.) and job roles.

⚠️

Barriers Assessment

Uncovers housing, transport, or childcare needs that impact retention.

Data Handling: Raw responses are stored in the Protected Zone.
Computed scores and "Readiness" summaries are written to the Application Zone for matching.

Step 3

Individualized Workforce Plan

Plan Components

  • Long-term Goal: The target career outcome (e.g., CNA Certification).
  • Short-term Milestones: Incremental steps toward the goal.
  • Actionable Tasks: Individual items with due dates and owners.
  • Medicaid Encounters: Case management notes linked directly to plan progress.

Engagement is bidirectional. Staff manage the plan, and participants own their tasks.

Trust & Safety

Participant Data Governance

Protected Zone

Restricted PHI/PII and 42 CFR Part 2 data. Encrypted demographics. Requires explicit consent for access.

Application Zone

Operational data (Plans, Tasks, Outcomes). Safe for aggregate reporting and dashboard visualization.

"Every byte of participant data is governed by Row-Level Security and a full audit trail."
